# Directions

Pick ONE. Each direction is a complete set of decisions. Blending directions is how sites drift back into generic. If the user has an existing brand, pick the direction closest to it and swap in their colors and fonts.

Font suggestions are free (Google Fonts or open licences) unless marked. Always self-host or use `font-display: swap`, and load only the weights you use.

---

## 1. Editorial

**Feel:** literate, confident, considered. Think a good magazine.
**Best for:** consultancies, writers, portfolios, publications, premium restaurants, law and finance boutiques.

- **Type:** high-contrast serif display (Fraunces, Newsreader, Instrument Serif, Playfair Display at large sizes only) + quiet sans for UI (Inter Tight, Geist, Söhne if licensed).
- **Scale:** dramatic. Display 72 to 120px on desktop; body 18 to 20px.
- **Palette:** paper `#f7f4ee`, ink `#1b1a17`, one muted accent (oxblood `#7a2e2a`, deep green `#2f4a3a` or ink blue `#1f3a5f`).
- **Radius:** 0 to 2px. **Shadows:** none.
- **Layout:** asymmetric 12-column grid, generous margins, pull quotes, rules (1px lines) as structure.
- **Signature move:** an oversized serif headline set left with a narrow column of body beside it.
- **Motion:** almost none; simple opacity on load.

## 2. Swiss / International

**Feel:** precise, rational, unshowy.
**Best for:** agencies, architects, engineering firms, data products, B2B tools.

- **Type:** one grotesk in several weights (Inter Tight, Neue Haas if licensed, Geist, Schibsted Grotesk). No serif.
- **Scale:** big jumps; heavy use of weight contrast (400 vs 700).
- **Palette:** white `#ffffff`, near-black `#111111`, one primary (signal red `#e3342f`, or a brand blue). Greys from a single hue.
- **Radius:** 0. **Shadows:** none.
- **Layout:** strict visible grid, flush-left ragged-right text, numbers and labels as design elements.
- **Signature move:** large numerals or an index (01, 02, 03) organizing content.
- **Motion:** linear, short, functional.

## 3. Quiet Luxury

**Feel:** restrained, expensive, slow.
**Best for:** hospitality, fine dining, fashion, real estate, wellness, jewellery.

- **Type:** elegant serif or refined sans with wide tracking for small caps (Cormorant Garamond for display, Jost or Manrope for text).
- **Scale:** moderate sizes, lots of air; small text set with care.
- **Palette:** bone `#f2efe9`, charcoal `#2b2a28`, one metallic-feeling neutral accent (bronze `#8a6a46`, stone `#9a958c`). Never gold gradients.
- **Radius:** 0. **Shadows:** none.
- **Layout:** large full-bleed photography, very few words per screen, wide margins.
- **Signature move:** a single photograph doing the work of the whole section, with a small caption.
- **Motion:** slow crossfades (600 to 900ms) on imagery only.

## 4. Warm Craft

**Feel:** human, tactile, local, honest.
**Best for:** cafes, bakeries, independent restaurants, makers, education, community organisations.

- **Type:** friendly serif or soft grotesk (Recoleta if licensed, Fraunces with soft axis, Bricolage Grotesque) + readable sans (Source Sans 3, Nunito Sans).
- **Palette:** cream `#fbf6ee`, cocoa `#3a2a20`, one warm accent (terracotta `#c2562f`, mustard `#c9952b`, olive `#6b7a3a`).
- **Radius:** 6 to 10px, or organic shapes. **Shadows:** soft, low, rare.
- **Layout:** relaxed grid, hand-feel details (a drawn underline, a stamp, real paper texture used once).
- **Signature move:** real photography of hands, food or materials, slightly warm graded.
- **Motion:** gentle, a little personality on hover, nothing bouncy on serious info.

## 5. Technical Mono

**Feel:** exact, developer-native, honest about being a tool.
**Best for:** dev tools, APIs, infrastructure, AI products that want credibility over hype.

- **Type:** grotesk (Geist, IBM Plex Sans) + monospace (Geist Mono, JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono) for labels, numbers, code.
- **Palette:** dark `#0c0d0e` or light `#fafafa`; neutrals from one hue; one sharp accent (acid green `#b5f23d`, signal orange `#ff6a13`) used tiny.
- **Radius:** 4 to 6px. **Shadows:** none; 1px borders define surfaces.
- **Layout:** dense, information-rich, real code and real output on the page.
- **Signature move:** a live or realistic terminal or code sample as the hero, not a glowing mockup.
- **Motion:** fast (120 to 200ms), crisp, no springs.

## 6. Bold Brutal

**Feel:** loud on purpose, confident, memorable.
**Best for:** creative studios, music, events, streetwear, campaigns.

- **Type:** a heavy display face (Anton, Archivo Black, Space Grotesk at 700, Clash Display if licensed) used huge.
- **Palette:** two strong colors and black, used in big flat fields.
- **Radius:** 0. **Borders:** thick (2 to 4px), visible.
- **Layout:** broken grid on purpose, overlapping elements, oversized type cropped by the viewport.
- **Signature move:** a headline so big it becomes the image.
- **Motion:** snappy, can be playful; still respect reduced motion.
- **Warning:** brutal is not careless. Alignment and spacing still need to be exact.

## 7. Soft Modern

**Feel:** approachable, calm, product-led. The good version of SaaS.
**Best for:** consumer apps, health, fintech, booking platforms, ordering systems.

- **Type:** a humanist or geometric sans with character (Manrope, Plus Jakarta Sans, DM Sans, General Sans) for everything.
- **Palette:** off-white `#f8f8f6`, slate ink `#1f2328`, one saturated but not violet accent (forest `#1f7a4d`, cobalt `#2747d6`, coral `#e8604c`), plus one tint of it for surfaces.
- **Radius:** 10 to 14px, consistent. **Shadows:** one soft elevation for floating elements only.
- **Layout:** clear sections, real product UI at real size, short copy.
- **Signature move:** the actual product, cropped to the moment of value (the order confirmed, the booking made).
- **Motion:** smooth ease-out, 200 to 300ms.
- **Warning:** this is the direction closest to slop. Run the scanner hard.

## 8. Monochrome Gallery

**Feel:** minimal, artful, the work speaks.
**Best for:** photographers, designers, artists, architects, product portfolios.

- **Type:** one neutral sans or one serif, small and quiet.
- **Palette:** white or very dark grey; no accent at all, or the accent comes from the work.
- **Radius:** 0. **Shadows:** none.
- **Layout:** the work is 80% of the page; text is captions.
- **Signature move:** an index or list view of projects that is itself beautiful typography.
- **Motion:** image reveal on load only.

## Choosing

| If the three adjectives include... | Lean toward |
|---|---|
| considered, literate, authoritative | Editorial |
| precise, rational, systematic | Swiss |
| refined, exclusive, calm | Quiet Luxury |
| warm, local, handmade, friendly | Warm Craft |
| technical, exact, credible | Technical Mono |
| loud, rebellious, energetic | Bold Brutal |
| approachable, simple, helpful | Soft Modern |
| minimal, artful, visual | Monochrome Gallery |

Write the choice in `DESIGN.md` with one sentence on why. If two directions seem equal, pick the one that is further from Soft Modern.
